# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard

## Overview

The **Superstore Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project built in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze retail sales performance across multiple dimensions. The dashboard transforms transactional data into meaningful business insights through executive KPIs, trend analysis, customer segmentation, profitability analysis, and strategic recommendations.

Designed with business decision-makers in mind, the report provides an end-to-end overview of sales performance while enabling users to explore trends, compare performance across categories, and identify opportunities for operational improvement.

---

## Objectives

This project aims to:

- Monitor overall business performance using executive KPIs.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate profitability across product categories.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare customer segment performance.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance through:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Profit
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Profit by Product Category
- Top Selling Products
- Geographic Sales Distribution
- Customer Segment Distribution

---

### 2. Performance Analysis

Compares business performance across different dimensions including:

- Year-over-Year Sales Performance
- Customer Segment Sales vs Profit
- Discount vs Profit Margin Analysis
- Shipping Mode Sales Distribution
- Product Subcategory Profitability

---

### 3. Executive Insights

Summarizes key business findings into actionable recommendations by highlighting:

- Sales seasonality and growth patterns
- Product category profitability
- Customer segment opportunities
- Operational and shipping insights

This page is intended for executives who need concise, business-focused recommendations rather than raw data.

## Business Insights

### Strategic Growth

Sales demonstrate consistent long-term growth with significant seasonal spikes during the fourth quarter, indicating strong holiday demand.

---

### Product Performance

Technology generates the highest profitability while Furniture produces comparatively lower profit despite strong sales, suggesting opportunities to review pricing, discount strategies, or operational costs.

---

### Customer Segments

The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of total sales, followed by Corporate and Home Office customers.

---

### Shipping Analysis

Standard Class accounts for the highest sales volume, indicating that customers generally prioritize standard shipping options over expedited delivery.
